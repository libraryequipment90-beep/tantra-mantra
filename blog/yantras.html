Understanding the Power of Yantras

March 28, 2025 · 6 min read

A yantra is a sacred diagram. Where a mantra is sound, a yantra is form. Together they hold a specific energy, the way a lamp holds flame. Sri Yantra, Kuber Yantra and Navgraha Yantra are among the better-known forms, but each should be chosen for a real purpose, not as decoration.

What a yantra actually is

The lines, triangles, lotus petals and bindu are not random art. They map a field of attention. When a yantra is energized with the matching mantra and kept with respect, it becomes a point of focus in the home, like a still centre in moving water.

How to keep a yantra at home

  • Place it on a clean, raised surface, facing east or north
  • Do not keep it on the floor or in a bedroom cupboard full of clothes
  • Wipe it gently and light a diya nearby on a regular day
  • Avoid buying random metal plates online without guidance

Yantra is not a shortcut

A yantra cannot replace honest effort, medical care or legal action. It supports the atmosphere in which you work. If someone sells you a yantra with a guarantee of overnight wealth or control over another person, walk away. Genuine tantra is about balance, not force.
