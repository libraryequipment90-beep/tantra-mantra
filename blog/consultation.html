How to Prepare for a Tantra Consultation

March 12, 2025 · 5 min read

A good consultation is a conversation, not a performance. You do not need to know Sanskrit or already believe in every ritual. You only need to describe your situation honestly.

What to keep ready

  • Your full name and a working mobile number
  • The main problem in one or two sentences
  • When it started and what you have already tried
  • Birth details if you have them, though they are not always required

What happens in the first call

We listen first. Then we tell you whether the case looks like a personal, family, planetary or energetic issue. If a ritual is needed, its duration and nature are explained before anything begins. If the issue is better handled by a doctor, counsellor or lawyer, we will say so.

Your privacy

Names, photos and problem details stay confidential. Do not send money to unknown numbers claiming to be us. Use only +91 93116 29296 or the form on this website.
