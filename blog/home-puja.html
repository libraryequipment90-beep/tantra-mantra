How to Perform a Simple Home Puja

April 12, 2025 · 6 min read

Home puja does not need a large temple or complicated ritual. What matters is cleanliness, a quiet mind and regular practice. Even ten sincere minutes every morning can change the feeling of a house.

1. Prepare a clean space

Choose one corner that will stay dedicated to worship. Wipe the surface, keep a small cloth, a diya, incense, a water pot and a photo or murti of the deity you follow. Do not keep the altar near shoes, dustbins or a noisy TV.

2. Bathe and sit with a calm mind

Wash your hands and face, or bathe if possible. Sit facing east or north. Take three slow breaths. The first offering of puja is attention, not flowers.

3. Light the diya

Light a ghee or oil lamp and a stick of incense. The flame represents agni, the witness of the ritual. Keep the diya on a stable plate, away from curtains and children.

4. Offer water, flower and a short prayer

Sprinkle a little water before the deity, place a flower or rice, and fold your hands. You may chant Om Namah Shivaya, Gayatri, or any family mantra 11 or 21 times. Speak in your own language if you do not know Sanskrit. Honesty is more important than perfect pronunciation.

5. Close with gratitude

Ask for protection of the family, clarity of mind and strength to do the right thing. Bow, sit quietly for a minute, then extinguish incense safely. Keep the diya burning only while you are nearby.

If your home feels unusually heavy even after daily puja, a personal consultation can help identify whether the issue is routine stress or a deeper energetic disturbance.
