Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process.

How do I book a consultation?

Fill the consultation form on this page or call +91 98765 43210. Our expert will connect with you within a few hours and schedule a private session.

Are your services really effective?

Our methods are rooted in authentic Tantra and Vedic practices, personalized after understanding your situation. Thousands of clients have shared real, lasting results.

Is my information safe and confidential?

Yes. Every consultation is strictly confidential. Your identity, problem and ritual details are never shared with anyone.

Do you work online or only in Varanasi?

Both. Photo, name, problem details and a video call are enough for most cases. In-person rituals in Varanasi are arranged when needed.

Is payment required before the first talk?

The first consultation is free. If a ritual is recommended, fees and duration are explained clearly before you decide.

What is the duration of the rituals?

Most rituals take 3 to 11 days depending on the problem. Some complex cases may require a longer sadhana, which is explained before we begin.

How much time does it take to see results?

Many clients notice a shift within 7–21 days. Timing depends on the nature of the issue, planetary influences and your faith in the process.

Can I consult for multiple problems at once?

Yes. During consultation we assess all concerns together and recommend a combined or sequential remedy so the energy work stays balanced.

Can NRIs take your services?

Yes. We regularly guide clients in the Gulf, UK, USA and Canada through WhatsApp and video.

Will you guarantee 100% results?

No honest practitioner guarantees a date. We guarantee authentic method, confidentiality and sincere sadhana. Results vary with karma, timing and your cooperation.

What should I prepare before consulting?

A short timeline of the problem, any previous remedies tried, and date of birth if relevant. Stay in a quiet place for the call.
